How to Play

Welcome to Shooter Game!

Controls:

  • Move: Move mouse/touch to aim
  • Click/Tap: Shoot target
  • SPACE: Change gun
  • ESC: Pause game

Rules:

  • Hit target = +10 points
  • Miss = -5 seconds
  • Targets appear every 3 seconds

Levels:

  • Easy: 30 seconds
  • Medium: 20 seconds
  • Hard: 15 seconds
